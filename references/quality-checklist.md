# Quality Checklist

Inspect visually at normal viewing size and, when needed, zoom into the subject. A generated file existing on disk is not sufficient evidence.

## Source fidelity

- Exactly one dominant subject remains.
- The subject is identifiable at first glance.
- Silhouette, orientation, defining structure, and key color cue match the source.
- Any essential reflection or connected formation is complete and physically plausible.
- No duplicate, replacement, invented landmark, artificial symmetry, or unrelated object appears.

## Composition

- Roughly 70-80% of the canvas reads as quiet negative space.
- Vertical subjects occupy about 18-25% of height.
- Horizontal subjects occupy about 35-55% of width and generally under 20% of height.
- Placement feels intentional and slightly poetic, not mechanically centered.
- Subject is not cropped, cramped, or touching the frame.

## Background

- Background is seamless and subtly dimensional.
- Color is warm ivory, pearl gray, mist gray, or very pale cyan as intended.
- No dead flat white, harsh gradient, paper texture, room corner, horizon seam, floor line, frame, or border.

## Material and light

- Material treatment is restrained and supports source recognition.
- Highlights are soft; contrast is controlled.
- Shadow is faint, broad, and soft.
- No visible base, pedestal, tabletop, acrylic stand, or product-display setup.
- No excessive gloss, chrome, gemstone sparkle, plasticity, neon, or theatrical glow.

## Style exclusions

- No torn-paper or ripped-edge effect.
- No collage, scrapbook, poster typography, labels, icons, stamps, logo, or watermark.
- No decorative props or busy atmospheric scenery.
- The result is neither a simple pasted cutout nor ordinary e-commerce photography.

## Series check

- Background temperature matches across the set.
- Light comes from the same direction with comparable softness.
- Subject scale follows one consistent geometry-aware rule.
- Shadow softness and suspension height feel related.
- Contrast, abstraction level, and material vocabulary belong to one family.
- Each image still retains the unique identity of its own source.

## Delivery check

- Open every final image and inspect it.
- Confirm requested aspect ratio and dimensions where specified.
- Use clear filenames containing source identity and variant.
- Deliver only visually accepted outputs; regenerate or correct failures first.
